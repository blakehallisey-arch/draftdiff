# Decision: keep the queue in Postgres

## Context

We looked at moving the job queue off Postgres and onto a dedicated broker. The
main driver was throughput; we are running about 400 jobs a minute at peak and
the table is now the largest in the database.

## Decision

We are keeping the queue in Postgres for now.

## Rationale

- At 400 jobs a minute we are nowhere near the ceiling. The benchmark put the
  breaking point closer to 4,000.
- A broker we could leverage adds an operational surface nobody on the team
  currently runs.
- I think the real cost here is the table size, and partitioning by month solves
  that without a new dependency.

We will revisit at 2,000 jobs a minute, or if the partitioning work does not
hold.
