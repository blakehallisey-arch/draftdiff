Decision: the job queue stays in Postgres.

We looked at moving it onto a dedicated broker because we are running about 400
jobs a minute at peak and the table is the largest in the database.

At 400 a minute we are nowhere near the ceiling. The benchmark put the breaking
point closer to 4,000. A broker also adds an operational surface nobody on the
team currently runs. The real cost is the table size, and partitioning by month
solves that without a new dependency.

We revisit at 2,000 jobs a minute, or if the partitioning work does not hold.
